By using any of our Products, you agree to the collection, use, storage, and sharing of information as described in this Privacy Policy. This policy is intended to comprehensively describe how our Products handle user data, including data received through Chrome extensions and Google APIs, and applies in addition to any in-product disclosures.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of user data through our Products. Not every category applies to every Product; we only collect what is necessary for the feature you are using.

### 1.1 Account and contact information
Name, email address, phone number, and other contact details you provide when creating an account, contacting support, or completing a purchase.

### 1.2 Authentication and license information
License keys, session tokens, device identifiers used to validate your subscription, and account credentials (passwords are never stored in plaintext).

### 1.3 User-provided content
Text prompts, voice and audio recordings, uploaded files and images, notes, ideas, and other content you submit to our Products in order to receive a response, generate output, or use AI-powered features.

### 1.4 Web content (Chrome extensions only, on user-initiated actions)
When you explicitly invoke a feature of one of our Chrome extensions (for example, asking BrowserGPT to act on the current page, summarize content, or run an automation), the extension may access:
- The URL and title of the active tab
- The content (DOM/text) of the active tab
- A screenshot of the active tab
- Form field values you have explicitly chosen to act on

We do **not** continuously monitor or collect your browsing history in the background. Page content is read only as needed to fulfill the action you invoked, and only from the tab(s) you have directed the extension to act on.

### 1.5 Device and log data
Information your device sends when you use our Products, such as IP address, device name, operating system version, browser version, language, time zone, timestamps of activity, crash reports, and diagnostic logs.

### 1.6 Payment metadata
When you subscribe to a paid plan, our payment processors (see Section 4) handle your payment details. We receive only metadata such as subscription status, plan tier, transaction ID, and the last four digits of the payment instrument. **We do not store full credit or debit card numbers.**

### 1.7 Cookies and local storage
Cookies, local storage entries, and similar identifiers used to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and improve our Products. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies, but some features may not work without them.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data described above only for the following purposes:

- To provide, operate, and maintain the Products you have requested.
- To execute AI-powered features, including sending your prompts and any content you have explicitly attached to AI model providers in order to generate a response.
- To authenticate you, validate your license, and enforce subscription entitlements.
- To process payments, send transactional emails (receipts, security notices, account changes), and provide customer support.
- To diagnose, troubleshoot, and fix bugs, including via crash reports and diagnostic logs.
- To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
- To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and lawful requests from public authorities.
- To improve the quality of our Products in aggregate (for example, measuring feature usage). We do not use Chrome Web Store user data to train independent generalized AI/ML models.

We do **not** sell your personal data. We do **not** use your data for advertising, retargeting, ad personalization, or to determine your creditworthiness.

## 3. How We Store Your Data

- **In transit:** All data sent between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS.
- **At rest:** Account data, content you submit, and license/subscription records are stored on cloud infrastructure provided by Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Cloudflare, in encrypted databases and object storage.
- **Voice and audio:** Audio sent for transcription or real-time voice features is processed transiently to fulfill your request and is **not** retained beyond the time required to complete that request, except where logs are required for abuse prevention or legal compliance.
- **Diagnostic logs:** Server and crash logs are typically retained for up to 90 days for operational and security purposes, then deleted or aggregated.
- **Account data:** Retained for the lifetime of your account. When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law (for example, tax records).

## 6. Permissions Used by Our Chrome Extensions

Our Chrome extensions request only the permissions needed to provide their features. The categories below describe how each permission is used across our extensions; not every extension requests every permission.

| Permission | Why we use it |
|---|---|
| `tabs`, `activeTab`, `windows`, `tabGroups` | To read the URL/title of the tab you are currently acting on, switch tabs you have asked the extension to open, and group/manage tabs as part of automations you invoke. |
| `scripting`, `<all_urls>`, host permissions | To inject the content scripts that read and interact with the page when you explicitly invoke a feature on it (e.g., "summarize this page", "fill this form"). |
| `storage` | To store your settings, license key, and per-extension preferences locally on your device. |
| `webRequest`, `webNavigation` | To detect navigation events so the assistant can respond to page-level user actions you have invoked (e.g., waiting for a page to load before continuing an automation). |
| `notifications` | To notify you when a long-running task (e.g., an automation) finishes. |
| `sidePanel`, `offscreen` | To render the assistant UI in Chrome's side panel and to host audio/voice processing in an off-screen document. |
| `contextMenus` | To add right-click menu entries for invoking extension features on selected text or links. |
| `idle`, `power` | To pause background tasks when your device is idle and to keep long-running automations from being interrupted by sleep, only while a task you started is in progress. |
| `debugger` | Used only when you explicitly start a feature that requires programmatic page control (e.g., automated form filling or browser automation). The "this extension started debugging this browser" banner shown by Chrome confirms when this is active. |
